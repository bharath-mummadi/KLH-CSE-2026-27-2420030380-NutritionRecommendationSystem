Factual RAG Retrieval Workflow

The multi-stage grounding flow preventing unsupported dietary assertions.
Step 1
Profile Framing
Input biomarkers, age, gender, and anthropometrics are processed through clinical risk equations.
Step 2
Target Formulation
Ensemble predicts baseline energy expenditure; daily targets are partitioned into slot shares.
Step 3
Dual-Index Retrieval
Food.com recipe corpus & USDA FoodData Central reference tables queried for matching meal slots.
Step 4
Biomarker Hard Filters
Candidate recipes checked against clinical limits: carb cap for diabetes, sodium/fat cap for cholesterol.
Step 5
Grounded Selection
Highest scoring recipes selected with factual attribution based purely on stored nutrient data.

Ground-Truth Knowledge Base Sources in this Repository

  • Food.com Recipe Dataset (data/foodcom_catalog.json): 165 verified recipes with complete nutrition breakdowns, ingredients, instructions, and user reviews.
  • USDA FoodData Central / NHANES Reference (data/NHANSE/train.csv): 8,618 laboratory nutrient records providing benchmark macro and micronutrient baselines.
